## OQ-3 resolution (`spawnPressureDeferred` reset on successful spawn)

ADR-231 §Open questions originally leaned **yes** (counter is "consecutive failures"; success should reset).

**Implementation resolution (architecturally moot)**: The 5-step algorithm in `src/core/orchd-spawn.ts:289-296` stamps `spawned_at = unixepoch()` on exit 0 but does **not** explicitly reset `spawnPressureDeferred`. This is correct by construction:

- Step 2 of §D2 is the dedup gate: `spawned_at IS NOT NULL → skipped-already-spawned`.
- Once successful spawn stamps `spawned_at`, every subsequent invocation of the handler for that epic short-circuits at step 2.
- `incrementSpawnPressureDeferred` (line 349) is therefore unreachable after first success for the epic-team's lifetime.
- If the operator manually re-enables spawn by clearing `spawned_at` (e.g. cage was dissolved & epic re-readied), they should also reset the counter as part of the same explicit reset — operator-owned, not handler-owned.

This makes the explicit reset a redundancy rather than a correctness requirement. **ADR-231 amended in-place** in `4f5ad16` companion edit (this signoff commit pairs the OQ-3 disposition into ADR-231 §Open questions header — see below).
